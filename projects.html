MHR Mesh Viewer
Aug 2026 — ongoing  ·  C++, OpenGL, Qt, Python
Tools for exploring and controlling the MHR parametric human model, in two parts: (1) an interactive Qt/OpenGL viewer with PCA shape and pose sliders and real-time mesh deformation; (2) measurement-driven body fitting in Python: the user enters target bust, waist and hip measurements, and the model adjusts interactively by recomputing the Jacobian of these measurements with respect to the PCA shape parameters in real time.

Avatar Playground
Jul 2026 — ongoing  ·  C++, OpenCV, OpenGL
A personal C++ framework for exploring monocular visual odometry and human mesh recovery. Phase 1 (feature tracking, camera pose estimation, sparse 3D mapping) is complete: on the TUM RGB-D fr1/xyz benchmark it reaches 84.3% frame-pair tracking success and 2.02° mean rotation error. Phase 2 integrates a parametric human mesh estimator.
