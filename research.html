Research

I work on how to recover and represent the human body in 3D. My current interests are parametric human body models, 2D-to-3D reconstruction, and human body tracking. My earlier research explored games, VR, and players' physiological signals.

Publications

Play With Your Emotions: Exploring Possibilities of Emotions as Game Input in NERO
Valérie Erb, Haesoo Kim, Tatiana Chibisova, Jeongmi Lee, Young Yim Doh  ·  CHI '22 Extended Abstracts (Student Game Competition)
A game concept that uses the player's emotional state as an active input, mapping it to representative in-game characters. Our proof-of-concept used heart-rate as a proxy for emotion; the mechanics were revised and evaluated through repeated player tests. Finalist at the CHI 2022 Student Game Competition.

Degree Projects

Promoting Environmental Awareness through Immersive VR Gameplay
M.S. Final Project (coursework track)  ·  KAIST, Graduate School of Culture Technology  ·  2023
A VR adventure game that teaches players about space debris and sustainability through storytelling and interactive mini-games, developed as a team project with Oh Ha-eun and Ryu Ji-seung. I built the procedural planet generation (HLSL, C#), tethered-net physics simulation, level design and visual design, and designed and analyzed the user study. The game, Space Cleaners, won the Grand Prize at the 2023 Korea Metaverse Dev Contest (2023 메타버스 개발자 경진대회).

Development of Methods and Algorithms for Fractal Modeling for Dynamic 3D VR Systems
B.S. Thesis  ·  HSE University, Moscow  ·  2021
A three-person thesis project that built a Unity fractal generator for classes of structures (interiors, architecture, landscapes, flora, cities) and rendered them as stereo 360° VR video. I built the raymarching renderer and SDF fractal library (HLSL, C#), Diamond-Square terrain, the "Interior" and "Landscape" classes, and the application UI and integration. A pilot study with heart-rate (ECG) and skin-response sensors produced frame-rate and stereo-separation guidelines for comfortable fractal VR video.
